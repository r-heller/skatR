# skatR: Analysis and Simulation of the Card Game Skat

A toolkit for the German card game Skat. Provides a configurable
card-play engine with suit, grand and null scoring, bidding-value
computation, descriptive ruleset metadata, and players ranging from
rule-based heuristics through determinized Monte Carlo search to an
exact double-dummy solver, Monte Carlo simulation over many deals, and
tidy analysis and visualisation of outcomes. Card play, scoring and
game-value calculation follow the standard 32-card Skat deck.
